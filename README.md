# Stock-AI: AI-Integrated Stock Trading Platform

## Background
Stock-AI was developed during the Spring 2026 semester for COM S 4020 at Iowa State University. We worked closely with our client, Dr. Hung Phan, a postdoctoral research scientist, to create an AI-integrated mock stock trading platform for education and research. Additionally, our project can be found on the [Iowa State Senior Design Webpage](https://seniord.cs.iastate.edu/2026-March-14/).

## Problem
Stock trading is a difficult skill to learn. The stock market is constantly changing and 
evolving, and gaining intuition on smart investments is time-consuming and difficult to 
test without making real investments. Learning platforms exist, but they are expensive 
services that add on to the already financially risky task of trading.

## Solution
Our platform, Stock-AI, aims to help beginners learn stock trading completely for free 
with no risk. It accomplishes this in two ways: first, the Stock-AI platform doesn’t charge 
users. There is no subscription or service cost to the user, and all trades are simulated 
to eliminate the risk of financial loss. Second, the platform includes AI integration to 
allow the user to ask questions and get summaries of relevant information, as well as to 
get recommendations on any given stock.

## Requirements
Our team was initially tasked with identifying important features from existing platforms 
such as BunnyQuant and Robinhood. We created an initial proposed list of features
that guided our development of Stock-AI. We compiled the list and gave it to Dr. 
Phan, who gave us the green light on them. While the final Stock-AI application has 
many of the same ideas, it is not a complete implementation of the initial feature list; as a 
team, we continually gained domain knowledge and evolved what we included, given 
the approval of Dr. Phan on new features. The initial feature document includes user 
security, portfolios, mock trading, and stock history viewing, but notably does not include 
the leagues feature (described below). The original proposed feature list can be found in the [Documents folder](/Documents/).
